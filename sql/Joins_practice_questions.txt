3.WAQTD emp name and dept names and their respective department names from emp and dept tables 
how many employees are working in each department
Q:select dept.department_name,COUNT(emp.employee_name) from emp INNER JOIN dept ON emp.department_id = dept.department_id group by dept.department_name;
