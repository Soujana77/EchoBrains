SQL JOINS

A JOIN is used to combine data from two or more tables based on a related column.

For example, suppose we have these two tables.

employees
id	name	dept_id
1	Soujanya	101
2	Rahul	102
3	Priya	103
4	Arun	101
5	Sneha	104
departments
dept_id	dept_name
101	IT
102	HR
103	Finance
105	Marketing

Notice:

Employee dept_id = 104 has no matching department
Department 105 has no matching employee

This makes it easier to understand the different JOINs.

Types of joins :

1.cross join 
2.inner join 
3.left join
4.right join 


3.Right join returns all records from right side tables and matching records from the left table 
Syntax:
SELECT t1.c1,t2.c2 
FROM t1 RIGHT JOIN t2
ON t1.comman_col = t2.comman_col;
