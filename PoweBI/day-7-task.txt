Phase 1: Data Cleaning (Power Query)
	Trim & Clean Whitespace: Remove leading/trailing spaces from Customer_Name and Region.
	Capitalization Standardizing: Convert Customer_Name and Region to Proper Case (e.g., john DOE → John Doe, south → South).
	Column Splitting: Split Product_Details by the pipe symbol (|) into two separate columns: Product_SKU and Product_Name.
	Data Type Conversions:
	Clean Unit_Price by removing the $ currency sign and converting it to Fixed Decimal Number.
	Convert Discount to Decimal Number after replacing "N/A" with 0.
	Convert Order_Date into a standard Date type (handle mixed date formats).
	Missing Value Handling:
	Impute or fill missing Units_Sold (e.g., replace null or blanks with 1 or column average).
	Handle missing Region values (e.g., replace blank with "Unknown").
Phase 2: Text & Date Calculated Columns
	Customer Initials: Create a column extracting customer initials (e.g., John Doe → JD).
	Category Extraction: Extract the category code from Product_SKU (e.g., PROD-ELEC-101 → ELEC).
	Date Attributes: Extract Year, Month Name, Quarter, and Day of Week from Order_Date.
	Gross Revenue Column: Calculate Gross_Sales = Units_Sold * Unit_Price.
	Net Sales Column: Calculate Net_Sales = Gross_Sales * (1 - Discount).
