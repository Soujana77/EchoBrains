DAX-Data Analysis Expression.
it is the formula language behind power BI,excel,Powerpivot,and analysis sevices .If you already know excel formulas.some of the syntax willlook familiar right away.

teh key mental shift:DAX thinks in tables and columns, never single cells. An excel formula acts on one cell at a time .a DAX formula acts on an entire column or table and recalcylates automatically based on whatever filters are currently applies to a report.

*Used to build measures and calculated columns
*Operates on entire tables and columns, not individual cells.
*Same language across Power BI, Excel for data analysis
